Detailed Baking instructions for making a bread

  1. Preheat your oven

    Before you start baking preheat your oven to the temperature specified in your bread recipe. That is typically around 190 degree celsius to 220 degree celsius depending on the type of bread.

  2. Prepare your dough

    Follow your bread recipe to prepare the dough. This usually involves mixing ingredients, kneading the dough and allowing it to rise.

  3. Shape the dough

    Once the dough has risen, shape according to your loaf shape or rolls.

  4. Final proofing

    Allow the shaped dugh to undergo it's final rise[proofing]. This usually takes place on a baking sheet or in a loaf pan covered with a clean kitchen towel or plastic wrap.

  5. Slash the dough [optional]

    Some bread recipe benefit from slashing the top of the dough just before baking. This helps control the expansion of the dugh during baking and can create an attractive pattern on the finished loaf.

  6. Bake

    Place the shaped and proofed dough in the preheated oven. Bake according to the recipe instructions, usually for 20 to 40 minutes, depending on the size and type of bread.

  7. Check for doneness

    Bread is typically done when it is golden brown on the outside and sounds hollow when tapped on the bottom. You can also a digital thermometer to check the internal temperature, it should register around 88 degree celcuis to 93 degree celsuis for most bread types.

  8. Cooling

    Once baked remove the bread from the oven amd let it cool on a wire rack for at least 15 minutes before slicing. This allows the bread to finish setting and prevents it from becoming too dense.

  9. Enjoy

    Once cooled slice and enjoy your freshly baked bread!
Note; Remember to always follow the specific instructions provided in your personal bread recipe if there's any.
